# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Pistis harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive
feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy.
Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, sexualized attention, and publishing
others' private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their
verified GitHub profile. Maintainers will investigate fairly and protect the
reporter's privacy. Temporary or permanent participation restrictions may be
applied proportionately.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
