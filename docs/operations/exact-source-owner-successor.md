# Review an internal owner exception

This flow requires the matching qualified Monas, Jenkins and Proxenos source
and the phone's existing current enrolment and ACK registration. Readiness or
source publication alone does not establish those prerequisites. Use the
existing enrolled HTTPS origin with its normal pinned transport.

In Pistis Settings, open **Review source qualification**, then **Load current
request**. When the current authority reports the prepared subject requires an
internal owner exception, choose **Review the required internal owner
exception** on that same screen. Review every governance field and the full
bound originals, then explicitly approve with Face ID. After its retention
receipt, load the source review on that same screen and review/sign that
separate task. The original session, subject and presentation deadline are
preserved; navigating away cancels the operation. Governance approval does not
silently approve the technical review.

Both actions use the existing ACK key and a fresh Face ID ceremony. The app
creates or imports no key. The original thirty-second presentation budget and
all ancestor expiries still apply. Expiry, cancellation, backgrounding or
uncertain delivery stop the attempt; a restored screen or a new session does
not revive it. The server independently repeats current native audit and
selected ACK checks, and Prox repeats its own current checks before admission.

The exact adopted protocol and its acceptance record are
[the supplement](../protocol/exact-source-owner-successor-witness-v1.rst) and
[the adoption record](../protocol/exact-source-owner-successor-adoption.json).
The source disposition is separate from a protected merge and genuine CI.
Simulator or component tests do not establish physical phone acceptance,
installation, custody or provider authority.
